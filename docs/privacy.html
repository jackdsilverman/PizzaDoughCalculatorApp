Privacy Policy

Pizza Dough app · Effective November 11, 2026

Pizza Dough does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app does

All recipe calculations happen on your device. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos, or any other device data.

Data collection

  • No personal data is collected.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are included.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.
