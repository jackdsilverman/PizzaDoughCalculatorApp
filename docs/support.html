Get help or report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/jackdsilverman/PizzaDoughCalculatorApp/issues. Include your device, OS version, and the settings you picked (style, leavening, number and size of pizzas) so the problem can be reproduced.

Common questions

Why don't yeast or starter amounts change with longer cold ferments?

The recipes use one tested amount for a cold ferment, whatever its length.

Why is the diameter always in inches?

Pizza sizes are almost always quoted in inches, so the size slider stays in inches even in metric mode. Ingredient weights follow your metric or imperial choice.

What equipment do the directions assume?

A stand mixer with a dough hook.
